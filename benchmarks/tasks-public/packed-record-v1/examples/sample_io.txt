Inputs and outputs of the protected library. Long or binary inputs are shown as hex.

input="aegis-ir"                       -> flags=1788695333 fold=15139351908703633203 accept=0

input="AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA"               -> flags=4055600329 fold=3670641339382699889 accept=0
input="example-record-payload"         -> flags=1788359969 fold=11226640532754294707 accept=0

Passing these examples is necessary, not sufficient: the grader also compares against hidden cases and a seeded fuzz corpus.
